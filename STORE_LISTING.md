# Store Listing

## App name
QR Scanner & Generator

## Short description
Fast QR scanner and QR code generator with local history and privacy-focused design.

## Full description
Scan and create QR codes quickly with a clean, lightweight Android utility.

### Scan QR codes
- Scan QR codes with your camera.
- Scan a QR code from an image.
- Use the flashlight when lighting is low.
- Copy, share, or open supported website results.
- Recognize common QR content types such as websites, Wi-Fi, contacts, phone numbers, email, SMS, and locations.

### Create QR codes
Create QR codes for text, websites, Wi-Fi, phone numbers, email, SMS, contacts, and locations. Generated QR images are high quality and can be saved or shared.

### History
Keep scanned and generated QR codes locally with timestamps. View, copy, share, delete individual entries, or clear all history.

### Privacy-focused
QR processing and generation are designed to happen on the device. No account is required, and the app does not provide a server for uploading QR contents. Camera permission is requested only when scanning.
