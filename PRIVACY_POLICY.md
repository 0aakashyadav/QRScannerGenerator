# Privacy Policy — QR Scanner & Generator

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

QR Scanner & Generator is designed to process QR information locally on the device.

## Information processed by the app

- Camera frames are processed on-device for QR detection when the user starts scanning.
- Images selected through the Android system picker are processed on-device for QR detection.
- QR values entered by the user or detected by the app can be stored locally in the app's history database when the relevant feature is used.
- Theme preference is stored locally.

## Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account and does not operate a server for QR data. We do not intentionally upload QR contents, contacts, location entries, phone numbers, email addresses, Wi-Fi passwords, or generated QR data to a developer-controlled server.

## Permissions

The app requests camera permission only for camera scanning. Gallery image selection uses the Android system picker and does not require broad storage permission. The app does not request contacts, microphone, location, phone-call, or SMS permissions.

## Advertising

The v2 development build does not integrate an advertising SDK and does not display ads. Monetization may be reconsidered for a future release, with the privacy policy updated before any advertising functionality is added.


## Sharing and opening content

When you choose Copy, Share, Save, or Open website, the app passes the selected content to the Android system or another app you explicitly choose. Those receiving apps have their own privacy practices.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual history entries or clear all QR history from inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data according to Android's normal application-data behavior.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The latest version should be published with the app's distribution materials.
